Privacy Policy – Scandal Shores

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Scandal Shores, a mobile game published by its developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

1. Information processed

Scandal Shores does not require you to create an in-game account to play. Game progress is designed to be stored locally on your device. However, third-party services integrated into the game may process limited information needed to provide advertising, in-app purchases, security, diagnostics, and related platform functionality.

2. Advertising

The game may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate technical information, diagnostics, and other data as permitted by your device settings and applicable law. Advertising identifiers and consent choices are controlled by the relevant platform and advertising services.

3. In-app purchases

The game may offer optional digital purchases. Payments are processed through Google Play. We do not directly receive or store your full payment-card details. Google Play may process transaction and purchase information according to Google's own policies.

4. Game data

Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored progress unless a platform backup mechanism is available.

5. Purposes

Information processed by the app or its service providers may be used to operate game features, deliver optional advertising, process purchases, maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, diagnose technical problems, and improve reliability.

6. Third-party services

The game may use Google Play services, Google Play Billing, and Google advertising services. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Children

Scandal Shores is not specifically directed to children under 13. Age eligibility and advertising treatment may also depend on local law and the settings and declarations used for distribution through Google Play.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Information processed independently by third-party providers is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the game and rely on established platform providers for payment and advertising functionality. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Scandal Shores, contact the developer using the developer contact email displayed on the game's Google Play listing.
